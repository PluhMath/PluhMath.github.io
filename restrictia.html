About Restrictia: Island Overdrive 3D

Full 3D open-world arcade kart racing across The Split Archipelago. Drive, drift, and boost across three distinct zones — the brutalist Restrictia Sector, the treacherous 5-Mile Divide ocean, and the sunny Crimson Island Mainland. Choose from 6 unique factory vehicles, each with a dedicated driver and Tomodachi Life-style voice synthesis. Seamlessly transform into a jetski in water or dive as a submarine through underwater tunnels. Toggle the Pit Stop system for fuel management or cruise with infinite energy in Free Roam.

Controls

How To Play

Select a vehicle and driver, then explore the open world freely. Use W/Up to accelerate, A/D to steer. Press Space to hop, then hold it while turning to initiate a drift — blue sparks mean a small boost on release, orange sparks mean a massive boost. Drive into any water to transform into a jetski. Hold Shift to dive underwater as a submarine, Ctrl to surface. Toggle Pit Stops in the pause menu to add fuel management — find glowing pit stop bays to refuel!
